## How to use *ggTimer*:
To access an already hosted version of *ggTimer*, go to [this website](https://ggtimer.pages.dev/).
Click the **Start** button to start / pause the timer — you can also do this with <kbd>SPACE</kbd>.
You can reset the timer with the **Reset** button — the shortcut for it is <kbd>Backspace</kbd> or <kbd>Delete</kbd>.

---

## Advanced Mode:
To toggle advanced mode, click the checkbox at the top of the page, or press <kbd>O</kbd>. This will open the *advanced options* menu.
This menu will keep a record of the times that you have recorded. To add a time to the list, simply press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> on a recorded time, and it will be recorded.
Click any item in the list, and it will be subsequently copied to your clipboard.

### More Options:
You can delete all of the recorded times in the list, but this process cannot be undone. You can also export your data into a `.csv` file, of which can also be opened inside of a spreadsheet app like *Microsoft Excel* or *Google Sheets*.
